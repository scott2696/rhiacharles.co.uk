Privacy Policy

Written by Olivia Williams · Fact-checked by James Wilson · Updated 4 September 2026

The short version: this site collects almost nothing. There is no account to create, no newsletter, no login and no advertising network. If you email us we keep the email; if you visit, our host keeps a standard server log. That is close to the whole story, and the detail is below.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for rhiacharles.co.uk is the site's operator, contactable at hello@rhiacharles.co.uk or through the contact page. This policy is written to meet the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

We do not collect: names (unless you put one in an email), postal addresses, dates of birth, payment details, gambling activity, account details at any operator, or any special category data. There is no registration, no login, no newsletter, no comment section and no user profile on this site, so there is no store of user records to collect anything into.

3. Analytics

We use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to see which pages are read. This measures page views, referring sites, approximate country and device type. It does not set advertising cookies, does not build a profile of you, does not track you across other websites, and does not share data with advertising networks. There is no Google Ads or Meta pixel on this site.

4. Affiliate links and what happens when you click one

Links to gambling operators are affiliate links. When you click one, you leave this site and arrive at the operator's site, usually carrying a tracking identifier in the URL so the operator knows the visit came from here and can pay commission if you open an account.

Two things follow, and they matter:

  • The tracking is done by the operator, not by us. The operator may set its own cookies and collect its own data under its own privacy policy, which we do not control and are not responsible for. Read it before registering.
  • We never receive your personal data from an operator. Affiliate reporting shows us aggregate numbers — clicks, registrations, revenue — not names, email addresses, deposits or gambling activity. We do not know who opened an account, and we could not find out.

5. Cookies

This site sets no advertising or profiling cookies of its own. What cookies are used, and by whom, is set out in the cookie policy.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for marketing. Data is processed by:

  • Our hosting provider, which processes server logs to deliver and secure the site.
  • Our email provider, which processes messages you send us.
  • Our analytics provider, which processes aggregate usage data.

Each acts as a processor on our instructions. Where a processor is outside the UK, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision.

We may also disclose data where required by law, or to establish or defend legal claims.

7. How long we keep things

  • Emails: 24 months from the last message in the exchange, then deleted. Where a message is evidence of a correction we published, the substance may be kept longer with personal identifiers removed.
  • Server logs: as long as our host retains them, typically under 30 days.
  • Analytics: retained in aggregate form only, not linked to an individual.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure of your data, where the grounds apply.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including at any time.
  • Portability of data you provided to us.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To exercise any of these, email hello@rhiacharles.co.uk with “Data request” in the subject line. We respond within one month, as required. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

In practice, unless you have emailed us, we are unlikely to hold anything about you at all — and we may ask for information to identify what, if anything, we hold, which we will not keep beyond answering.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at hello@rhiacharles.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Children

This site is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and it will be deleted.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Email is held in a provider account protected by multi-factor authentication. Because we hold so little — no accounts, no payment data, no user records — the practical risk to you from this site is very low, which is itself a deliberate design decision rather than an accident.

12. Changes to this policy

Any change is published here with an updated date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be noted on the about page.
