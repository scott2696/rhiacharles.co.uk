Cookie Policy

This site sets no advertising cookies, no profiling cookies and no cross-site tracking cookies of its own. That is why you are not looking at a consent banner. The one place cookies genuinely matter here is what happens when you click through to a gambling operator, and that is explained in full below.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, which the browser sends back on later requests. Cookies are how a site remembers a preference or recognises a returning visitor. Similar technologies — local storage, pixels, tracking parameters in a URL — do comparable jobs and are covered by the same rules under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, consent is required before storing information on your device unless the storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for. Since this site sets no advertising, profiling or analytics cookies of its own, there is nothing here that requires consent — so asking for it would be theatre.

If that ever changes, a consent mechanism will be added and this page will say so. We would rather remove the cookies than add the banner.

4. What happens when you click an affiliate link

This is the part worth reading.

Links to gambling operators on this site are affiliate links. When you click one:

  1. You leave rhiacharles.co.uk and go to the operator's site, or to its affiliate network's redirect, carrying an identifier in the URL that says the visit came from here.
  2. The operator or its network may then set cookies on your device. Those are the operator's cookies, set on the operator's domain, under the operator's privacy and cookie policies.
  3. If you later open an account, the identifier lets the operator attribute it to this site and pay commission.

Two things to be clear about. We do not set those cookies and we do not control them — read the operator's own cookie policy, which will be linked in its footer. And we never receive your personal data back. Affiliate reporting gives us aggregate figures: clicks, registrations, revenue. Not names, not email addresses, not deposits, not what you played. We do not know who opened an account and have no way of finding out.

5. Third-party content

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google's servers carrying your IP address as any web request does. No cookie is set by that request. Everything else — stylesheets, images, operator logos — is served from this site's own domain, so there are no embedded videos, social widgets, comment systems or ad tags to set third-party cookies.

6. How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in any browser. Blocking everything will not break this site, because nothing here depends on a cookie — though it will affect other sites, including gambling operators, where a blocked cookie may prevent login or a working cashier.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Block All Cookies, or Preferences → Privacy on macOS
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Private or incognito windows discard cookies when closed. Browser-level tracking protection — Safari's Intelligent Tracking Prevention, Firefox's Enhanced Tracking Protection — will block most third-party trackers on other sites automatically.

A control that matters more than cookies

If your concern is gambling sites tracking you or reaching you, browser cookie settings are a weak tool. The strong ones are a bank gambling block, which stops the payment, and BetBlocker, which is free and blocks the sites themselves including offshore ones. Both are on the responsible gambling page.
