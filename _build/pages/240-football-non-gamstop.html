Accumulators, and the compounding problem

The accumulator is the most British bet there is, and it is worth understanding what it costs.

Margins multiply across legs. If each leg carries a 6% overround, the effective margin on the accumulator is roughly 1.06 raised to the number of legs, minus one. Which gives:

Two honest observations. First, this is why acca insurance, free bet clubs and enhanced multiples exist: they refund a fraction of a bet whose margin is enormous, which is cheap for the book and feels generous. Second, none of this means you should not place one. A £5 six-fold on a Saturday is a legitimate purchase of a few hours of interest in matches you would otherwise ignore. Just price it as entertainment rather than as an investment, because the bookmaker certainly has, and keep the stake at the level you would happily spend on the entertainment alone.
