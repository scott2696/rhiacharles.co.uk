What this site is

rhiacharles.co.uk is an independent guide to online casinos and betting sites available to players in the United Kingdom. It is written by James Wilson and fact-checked by Olivia Williams. There are two of us. There is no content agency, no freelance pool, and no syndicated material anywhere on the site — which is why there are twenty-odd pages here rather than two hundred.

The organising idea is simple enough to state in a sentence: publish the evidence, not the conclusion. Every ranking on this site is produced by a scoring model whose weights are published, applied to data that is also published. If you disagree with a weight, you have everything you need to recalculate the ranking your own way, and I would rather you did that than took my word for it.

Why it exists

I spent seven years in payments and fraud operations at a UK-licensed gambling operator, four of them on the team that signed off withdrawal queues. From that seat you learn two things very quickly. The first is that most slow payouts are boring — a compliance queue staffed in office hours, a card scheme's settlement cycle, a document rejected because a corner was cropped. The second is that none of that is visible to the customer, who experiences it as a company deciding whether to give them their money back.

The gap between those two views is where this site lives. I cannot make an operator faster, but I can measure it, publish the measurements, and let you choose with the numbers in front of you.

How this site makes money

Affiliate commission. When somebody opens an account through a link on this site, the operator pays a share of its revenue from that account. It costs you nothing extra and it does not change your terms.

The rates run between 30% and 50% revenue share, and the rate for each operator is published on its individual review page. That is unusual and it is deliberate: the whole argument for trusting a ranking on an affiliate site is that you can check the ranking against the rates. Here is what that check shows:

  • The site I rank first pays the same rate as several sites I rank below it.
  • The two operators paying the lowest rate, 30%, sit tenth and eleventh — not because they pay less, but because they carry 40x wagering and the thinnest payout samples I hold.
  • The operator paying the highest rate, 50%, holds a sponsored slot at number two in my casino tables. That row is marked “Ad” and carries no score it did not earn — it scores below every other casino in the table, and the score column shows it sitting out of sequence.
  • Several operators paying the highest rate, 45%, sit in the bottom half.
  • I recommend declining the welcome bonus at four of the seven operators whose bonuses I model, including the one I rank second. There is no commission in that advice.

Ranking position is not for sale. A sponsored slot is, and the difference is the whole point. Where an operator has bought its position, the row says “Ad” and its score sits out of sequence with the rows around it, so you can always tell which is which by reading the score column. No operator sees a review before it is published, no operator has editorial input of any kind, and no payment has ever moved a scored position.

What this site will not do

A short list, because a stated boundary is worth more than a general claim of integrity:

  • It will not target self-excluded players. Every non-GamStop page on this site opens with a section telling readers who have self-excluded not to use these operators, with links to BetBlocker, Gamban and the National Gambling Helpline. That section costs money and it is not up for discussion.
  • It will not list an operator that markets at self-excluded players. One of the 42 sites opened this year did, and it was rejected on that basis alone regardless of its commission rate.
  • It will not publish a payout time it has not measured. Every median on this site is printed with the number of requests behind it, so a four-request figure is never dressed up as an eleven-request one.
  • It will not describe a bonus by its headline percentage. Every offer is priced through a published model, and where that model says the offer is bad, the page says the offer is bad.
  • It will not pretend offshore casinos are as safe as licensed ones. They are not, the reasons are specific, and they are listed in full on non-GamStop casinos.
  • It will not run an operator-supplied review. Everything is written here, by one of two named people.

Corrections policy

If something on this site is wrong, I want to know. Send it over with enough detail to check — a screenshot, a date, a link — and I will verify it against my own records and respond.

Corrections are made openly. A factual error is fixed and noted with a date on the page where it appeared. A change to a score or a ranking is noted on the withdrawal ledger with the reason for the change. Nothing is silently edited, because a review site that quietly rewrites its own history is not a review site.

Operators are welcome to dispute anything published here and are treated like any other correspondent: bring evidence and the page changes; bring a complaint and it does not.

Editorial standards

  • Named authorship on every page, with a named fact-checker who is never the same person.
  • First-hand testing. No operator is scored until an account has been opened with real details, funded with real money, played, and withdrawn from at least five times.
  • Published methodology. The five scoring weights are on how I review casinos and are applied identically to every operator.
  • Computed scores. Scores are calculated from the published weights when the site is built, not typed into a field. There is nowhere in this system for me to simply write a number I liked.
  • Published raw data. Every payout claim traces to the withdrawal ledger.
  • Stated uncertainty. Where a sample is small or a figure is unverified, the page says so rather than rounding it into confidence.
  • Dated updates. Every page carries its last update date. Rankings are reviewed monthly; the ledger is updated whenever a withdrawal completes.

Responsible gambling position

This site is funded by gambling. It would be absurd to pretend that puts me in a neutral position, so I will not.

What I can do is be accurate. Casino games carry a permanent mathematical edge in the operator's favour; over enough plays, that edge wins. Nothing on this site is a strategy for making money, and the pages that come closest — the bonus clearance model, the RTP work — are about spending less rather than winning more. The most valuable advice on the whole site is on the live casinos page, and it is that a £5 blackjack hand costs about a fortieth of a £5 slot spin.

If gambling has stopped being entertainment for you, the responsible gambling page lists every tool available in the UK, most of them free. The National Gambling Helpline is 0808 8020 133, free and confidential, 24 hours a day.
