## 6. Cannibalisation controls

Four pairs are close enough to need explicit separation, enforced by differing
H1s, intents and internal-link direction:

1. **`/` vs `/online-casinos/`** — commercial ranking vs informational hub. The
   hub links up to the homepage for "which one"; the homepage links down for
   "how it works".
2. **`/online-betting/` vs `/best-sports-betting-sites/`** — the guide owns
   informational betting terms and margin education; the ranking owns commercial
   "best betting sites" terms.
3. **`/non-gamstop-casinos/` vs `/`** — same operators, different question. The
   non-GamStop page is framed entirely around the trade-off, not the ranking.
4. **`/no-deposit-casinos/` vs `/non-gamstop-casinos-with-free-spins/`** —
   no-deposit offers vs deposit-triggered and recurring spins.
