James Wilson

Founder and lead reviewer

I spent seven years in payments and fraud operations at a UK-licensed gambling operator, four of them on the team that signed off withdrawal queues. That job is the reason this site exists. I spent four years watching perfectly ordinary withdrawals sit in a queue for reasons that were entirely explicable from the inside and completely invisible from the outside — and then I would go home and read casino review sites confidently describing payout speeds that nobody there had measured.

So I started measuring. I open every account on this site myself, with my own name and my own address, from a home broadband connection in Bristol. I deposit my own money. I read the full terms document before claiming anything, not the summary box. I request withdrawals at varying amounts and times of day, and I write down what time cleared funds actually appear. That record is public, in full, on the withdrawal ledger — 74 payouts across eleven operators, including the three-day ones and the four-request samples I would not lean on.

I write everything on this site except the legal and regulatory pages, which are Olivia's. My background means I am better than most at reading a cashier and worse than most at pretending a large number is a good offer.

How we work

Every page has a named author and a named checker. The two are never the same person. The byline at the top of each page shows both, along with the date it was last updated.

Nothing is outsourced. There is no content agency, no freelance pool and no syndicated material anywhere on this site. That is why there are twenty-odd pages rather than two hundred.

Testing is first-hand. A site does not get a score until an account has been opened, funded with real money, played, and withdrawn from at least five times. The full protocol is on how I review casinos and the raw data is on the withdrawal ledger.

Corrections are made openly. If something here is wrong, tell us with enough detail to check it. Factual errors are fixed and dated on the page where they appeared; changes to a score or ranking are noted on the ledger with the reason. Nothing is quietly rewritten.

We are not neutral about one thing. Every operator on this site is licensed offshore and none is connected to GamStop. If you have self-excluded, we would rather you closed the page than opened an account, and we say so at the top of every page in that category. That is a settled editorial position and it is not affected by commission rates.
