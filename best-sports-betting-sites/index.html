How do I compare betting sites properly?

Calculate the overround on markets you actually bet. Take each outcome, work out 1 divided by its decimal odds, and add them up; anything above 1.00 is the bookmaker's margin. Compare the same market at the same moment across two or three books. Headline promotions are worth far less than a persistently tighter price: 1.5 percentage points of margin on £2,000 of annual turnover is about £30, repeated every year, while a welcome offer happens once.

Is my money safe at an offshore bookmaker?

Less protected than at a UKGC-licensed one, and that is the honest answer. There is no requirement to segregate customer funds, no free binding dispute resolution through IBAS, and recourse in a dispute runs through an offshore regulator that is slower and less decisive. What you can check is the evidence: a licence that appears on the regulator's own register, published withdrawal limits, and a record of actually paying. All 74 withdrawals I have requested across these operators were paid.

Can I bet on horse racing at these sites?

Yes, with two real caveats. Overrounds on win markets run 12% to 21%, wider than football because every runner carries a slice of margin. And best odds guaranteed — where an early price is upgraded to the starting price if the horse drifts — is standard at UK bookmakers and not reliably offered here. If you bet racing at early prices, that absence costs you more than any bonus makes back.

What is the minimum bet at these sites?

Typically 10p to 50p per selection, with minimum deposits of £10 at TenoBet and £20 elsewhere. Minimum withdrawal matches the deposit minimum in each case. The more useful figure is the maximum: weekly withdrawal caps run from £6,000 to £10,000, which determines how long a large win takes to reach you.

Do I pay tax on my betting winnings?

No. Betting duty on punters was abolished in 2001 and replaced with a duty on operators, so winnings are not taxable income in the UK. There is no threshold, nothing to declare, and no distinction between UKGC-licensed and offshore books. Even full-time bettors are generally outside income tax on winnings, because gambling is not treated as a trade. My tax on gambling winnings page covers the detail.
